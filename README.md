# My Favorite Movie: Titanic
 
> "Winning that ticket, Rose, was the best thing that ever happened to me... it brought me to you." — Jack Dawson
 
## Overview
Titanic is a 1997 epic romance and disaster film directed, written, co-produced, and co-edited by James Cameron.
 
### Key Highlights
* **Main Characters:** Jack Dawson (played by Leonardo DiCaprio) and Rose DeWitt Bukater (played by Kate Winslet)
* **Setting:** The RMS Titanic, on its ill-fated maiden voyage in April 1912
* **Genre:** Romance, Drama, Disaster
 
### Why It's Great
1. A timeless and emotional love story set against a historic tragedy.
2. Groundbreaking visual effects and set design that recreate the legendary ship.
3. Iconic musical score and theme song ("My Heart Will Go On" by Celine Dion).
